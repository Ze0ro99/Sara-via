SARAVIA is designed around a premium, technology-enabled travel experience. Protecting personal information is an important part of building that experience. This policy describes the general principles that may apply when you interact with SARAVIA through its website, applications, services, booking flows, concierge experiences, or other digital products.

01

Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information that may be processed through SARAVIA's digital products and services, including travel discovery, reservations, transportation, concierge services, customer support, account management, payment-related functionality, and other features made available through the platform.

Certain services may be subject to additional privacy notices, terms, or disclosures provided at the point of collection or within the relevant service.

02

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use SARAVIA, the information processed may include categories such as:

  • Account information, such as your name, email address, telephone number, and account credentials.
  • Travel information, including destinations, dates, preferences, reservations, and service requirements.
  • Transaction and payment-related information required to process purchases, reservations, refunds, or other authorized transactions.
  • Device and technical information, such as browser type, operating system, IP address, device identifiers, and technical logs.
  • Usage information concerning interactions with the platform, features, pages, and services.
  • Information that you voluntarily provide when contacting customer support, requesting concierge assistance, or submitting feedback.
Data Minimization

SARAVIA should seek to process information that is reasonably relevant to the service being provided and avoid collecting information that is unnecessary for the stated purpose.

03

Pi Network Integration

Where SARAVIA provides functionality connected to Pi Network, certain information may be processed to support authentication, account association, transaction functionality, or other features enabled by the integration.

Depending on the specific functionality implemented, information may be exchanged between SARAVIA and relevant Pi Network services or infrastructure in accordance with the applicable technical requirements and permissions.

Users should review the applicable policies and terms of any third-party network or service before using an integrated feature.

04

How We May Use Information

Information may be used for legitimate operational, contractual, security, and service-related purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Providing travel, booking, transportation, concierge, and related services.
  • Processing authorized transactions and service requests.
  • Personalizing relevant experiences, recommendations, and service interactions.
  • Communicating with users regarding accounts, reservations, transactions, service updates, and support requests.
  • Maintaining platform functionality, reliability, performance, and security.
  • Detecting, investigating, and preventing fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and other security risks.
  • Improving products, services, user experience, and platform operations.
  • Complying with applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.
05

Information Sharing

SARAVIA may share or disclose information where reasonably necessary to operate its services, fulfill a user's request, protect users and the platform, or comply with applicable obligations.

Depending on the service, relevant information may be provided to categories of recipients such as:

  • Travel providers, accommodation partners, transportation providers, and other service providers involved in a requested booking or experience.
  • Payment processors and financial service providers involved in authorized transactions.
  • Technology, hosting, analytics, security, customer support, and infrastructure providers acting on behalf of the platform.
  • Relevant third-party platforms or networks where a user has requested or authorized an integrated service.
  • Government authorities, regulators, courts, or other parties where disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law.

SARAVIA does not treat user information as a commodity to be sold simply for its own sake. Any disclosure should be considered in the context of the applicable service, legal basis, user request, or operational requirement.

06

Information Security

SARAVIA is intended to use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards designed to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction of information.

Security measures may include access controls, encryption where appropriate, authentication mechanisms, monitoring, secure infrastructure, logging, and other protective technologies and procedures.

Important Security Notice

No internet-connected system, transmission method, or storage environment can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should also take reasonable steps to protect their credentials, devices, and account access.

07

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide requested services, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce applicable agreements, prevent abuse, meet legal or regulatory requirements, or otherwise fulfill legitimate operational purposes.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the purpose for which it was collected, and applicable legal or contractual requirements.

08

User Rights

Depending on the jurisdiction in which you are located, you may have rights concerning your personal information. These may include, where applicable:

  • Requesting access to personal information held about you.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Requesting deletion of information where legally applicable.
  • Requesting restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Objecting to certain forms of processing where applicable.
  • Requesting portability of certain information where applicable.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent, subject to applicable limitations.

Requests may be subject to identity verification and applicable legal limitations.

09

Cookies & Similar Technologies

SARAVIA may use cookies, local storage, pixels, analytics technologies, and similar mechanisms to support essential functionality, remember preferences, understand platform usage, improve performance, and enhance the user experience.

Depending on the technologies implemented and the jurisdiction involved, users may have options to manage certain cookies or similar technologies through browser, device, or platform settings.

10

Changes to this Privacy Policy

SARAVIA may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in services, technologies, operational practices, legal requirements, or other relevant circumstances.

When material changes are made, the updated version may be presented through the platform or other appropriate communication channels. The effective date should be reviewed whenever this policy is updated.

Current Version

This page represents the current published version of the SARAVIA Privacy Policy. The effective date should be added and maintained by SARAVIA before production launch.

11

Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, personal information, data requests, or privacy practices, please contact SARAVIA through the official contact channel provided within the platform.
